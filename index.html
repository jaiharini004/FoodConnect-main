How It Works

Four steps to make a difference

Whether you have food to share or need a meal, FoodConnect keeps it simple.

01

Donate Food

Have surplus food? Tell us where it is and how many people it can serve. Takes under a minute.

02

Connect

We match your donation with a verified community organization or a person in need nearby.

03

Deliver

The verified organization coordinates collection and ensures the food reaches the right people.

04

Make an Impact

Track your contribution. Earn community points. See the real difference your food made.
